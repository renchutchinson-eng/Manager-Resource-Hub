DEVELOP MY PEOPLE

Development Conversation Guide

A practical guide to help managers turn career and development conversations into meaningful actions.

A Simple Development Conversation

1. Start with aspirations

Ask what the employee wants to learn, strengthen or experience. Development does not always have to mean promotion.

2. Explore strengths

Discuss capabilities the employee already demonstrates and where those strengths could be used more effectively.

3. Identify a development priority

Agree one or two capabilities that would make the greatest difference to the employee's current or future contribution.

4. Turn development into action

Identify practical ways to build the capability through experience, coaching, exposure, learning or stretch assignments.

5. Agree follow-up

Confirm the action, who will support it and when progress will be discussed again.

Think Beyond Training

Development can happen through new responsibilities, project work, job shadowing, coaching, feedback, mentoring, problem-solving assignments and exposure to different parts of the business.

Keep Development Practical

  • Focus on one or two priorities at a time.
  • Connect development to real work wherever possible.
  • Be clear about what the employee owns.
  • Follow up rather than treating development as a one-time conversation.
  • Recognize progress and increased capability.

Questions to try

  • What would you like to get better at?
  • What kind of work gives you energy?
  • Where would you like more exposure?
  • What experience would stretch you?
  • What support would help?
  • What will you do next?

Next step

Capture agreed development priorities and actions in the employee's Individual Development Plan where appropriate.

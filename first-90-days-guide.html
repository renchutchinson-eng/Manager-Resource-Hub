LEAD WITH CONFIDENCE

First 90 Days Guide

A practical roadmap to help new managers build credibility, understand their team and establish strong leadership habits.

Your First 30 Days: Learn

  • Understand your team's responsibilities and priorities.
  • Meet individually with each team member.
  • Clarify expectations with your own manager.
  • Learn the key processes, policies and decision points relevant to your role.
  • Observe before making unnecessary changes.

Days 31–60: Align

  • Clarify team goals and priorities.
  • Establish regular 1:1s and team check-ins.
  • Identify strengths, capability gaps and support needs.
  • Address unclear expectations or recurring blockers.
  • Build relationships with key stakeholders.

Days 61–90: Lead

  • Set clear expectations and reinforce accountability.
  • Give regular feedback and recognition.
  • Begin meaningful development conversations.
  • Improve processes where there is a clear business need.
  • Review your progress with your manager.

What Good Looks Like by Day 90

  • Your team understands what you expect.
  • You understand your people's strengths and challenges.
  • Regular manager routines are established.
  • Key stakeholders know how to work with you.
  • You know when to act independently and when to seek support.

Build these habits early

  • Regular 1:1s
  • Clear expectations
  • Timely feedback
  • Follow-through
  • Good documentation
  • Early escalation when needed

Remember

You do not need to have every answer immediately. Strong managers ask questions, use available resources and seek guidance when appropriate.

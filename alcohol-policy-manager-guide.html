Alcohol Policy Manager Guide

A practical decision guide to help managers respond consistently and know when additional support may be required.

If a Concern Arises

1. Consider immediate safety

Determine whether there is an immediate risk to the employee, colleagues, customers or workplace.

2. Focus on what you observe

Record objective behaviours or circumstances rather than making assumptions about their cause.

3. Check the approved policy

Refer to the current company policy and applicable procedures before determining the appropriate management action.

4. Seek guidance where required

Involve the appropriate People & Culture and/or Health & Safety support where the situation requires specialist or formal guidance.

5. Document appropriately

Record relevant facts, actions taken, guidance received and any required follow-up.

Important Manager Principle

Respond to observable facts and safety concerns — not assumptions.

Managers should follow the approved company policy and seek appropriate guidance before taking formal action.

Quick decision check

  • Is anyone at immediate risk?
  • What have I actually observed?
  • What does the approved policy require?
  • Who needs to be involved?
  • What needs to be documented?

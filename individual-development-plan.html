Turn development priorities into clear actions, experiences and follow-up commitments.

What is an Individual Development Plan?

An Individual Development Plan helps an employee and manager agree what capability the employee wants or needs to strengthen and how that development will happen through practical actions and experiences.

Build the Plan

1. Development Priority

Identify the capability, skill or experience the employee needs to strengthen.

2. Desired Outcome

Describe what improved capability or performance should look like.

3. Development Actions

Agree practical actions such as stretch assignments, coaching, project work, job shadowing, mentoring or formal learning.

4. Support Required

Identify what support, access, feedback or resources the employee may need.

5. Review Progress

Agree when progress will be reviewed and how you will know the development has made a difference.

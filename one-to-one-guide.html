MANAGE MY TEAM

1:1 Conversation Guide

A practical guide to help managers have regular, meaningful conversations that support performance, engagement and development.

Purpose

Use this guide to structure regular one-to-one conversations with your team members. A strong 1:1 should create space for priorities, challenges, support and development — not just task updates.

A Simple 1:1 Structure

1. Check in

Start with the person, not the task list.

  • How are things going?
  • What has been going well?
  • Is anything affecting your ability to perform at your best?

2. Review priorities

  • What are you currently focused on?
  • Are there any blockers or competing priorities?
  • Are expectations and priorities clear?

3. Discuss performance and support

  • What is working well?
  • Where do you need more clarity or support?
  • Is there anything I can do differently as your manager?

4. Talk development

  • What are you learning right now?
  • Is there a skill or experience you would like to build?
  • Are there opportunities I can help create?

5. Agree next steps

  • What actions are coming out of this conversation?
  • Who owns each action?
  • When will we check in again?

Good 1:1s should be

  • Regular
  • Two-way
  • Focused
  • Honest
  • Action-oriented

Avoid

  • Turning every 1:1 into a status meeting
  • Doing all the talking
  • Waiting until something goes wrong to have a conversation
  • Making commitments and not following through

Quick Manager Check

Before you end the conversation, ask yourself:

  • Did I listen?
  • Did we clarify priorities?
  • Did we surface any issues?
  • Did we agree clear next steps?
  • Does the employee know what happens next?

Use this guide when

  • Holding regular manager check-ins
  • Reviewing priorities or workload
  • Discussing support needs
  • Talking about development
  • Strengthening manager–employee communication

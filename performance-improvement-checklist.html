Performance Improvement Checklist

A quick manager checklist for preparing, supporting and following up where an employee needs to improve their performance.

Before the Conversation

  • Identify the specific performance gap.
  • Confirm the expected performance standard.
  • Gather objective examples or evidence.
  • Consider previous feedback or support provided.
  • Identify what you need to understand from the employee.

During the Conversation

  • Explain the performance concern clearly.
  • Describe the expected standard.
  • Ask for the employee's perspective.
  • Listen for barriers, context or support needs.
  • Agree specific improvement actions.
  • Clarify what support will be provided.
  • Agree when progress will be reviewed.

After the Conversation

  • Document the key facts and agreed actions.
  • Provide any support or resources agreed.
  • Monitor progress against the agreed standard.
  • Recognize improvement where it occurs.
  • Hold the agreed follow-up conversation.

Keep it clear

The employee should leave the conversation understanding:


  • What needs to improve
  • What good performance looks like
  • What actions are expected
  • What support is available
  • When progress will be reviewed

Before formal action

Contact People & Culture where formal performance management may be required or where you are unsure of the appropriate next step.

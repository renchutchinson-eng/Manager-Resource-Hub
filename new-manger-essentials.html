Five Essentials for New Managers

1. Know Your Team

Understand each person's role, strengths, priorities, development needs and current challenges.

2. Set Clear Expectations

Make sure employees understand what is expected, why it matters and how good performance will be recognized.

3. Communicate Regularly

Establish consistent 1:1s, team check-ins and opportunities for two-way feedback.

4. Coach Early

Give timely feedback, recognize good performance and address concerns before they become larger problems.

5. Know When to Ask for Help

Use available policies, manager resources and specialist support when a decision is outside your authority or expertise.

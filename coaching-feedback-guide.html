Coaching & Feedback Guide

A practical guide to help managers give clear, constructive feedback and coach employees toward stronger performance and development.

Purpose

Use this guide to prepare for coaching and feedback conversations. Effective feedback helps employees understand what is working, what needs to change and what support is available.

A Simple Feedback Approach

1. Describe what you observed

Focus on the specific behaviour, action or outcome rather than assumptions about the person.

2. Explain the impact

Help the employee understand why the behaviour or outcome matters to the team, customer, process or business.

3. Invite their perspective

Ask questions and listen before deciding what needs to happen next.

4. Agree the way forward

Clarify what should continue or change and identify any support, coaching or resources required.

5. Follow up

Agree when progress will be reviewed and recognize improvement when you see it.

Example

Instead of:

“You need to communicate better.”


Try:

“During the last two team updates, the revised delivery timeline wasn't shared until after the deadline changed. That left the team with limited time to adjust. What was happening from your perspective?”

Good feedback should be

  • Specific
  • Timely
  • Based on observable facts
  • Two-way
  • Focused on improvement
  • Clear about next steps

Remember: coaching is not just for problems

Use coaching conversations to reinforce strengths, recognize good performance, build confidence and help employees prepare for future responsibilities.

Useful coaching questions

  • What do you think went well?
  • What would you do differently next time?
  • What is getting in the way?
  • What support would help?
  • What action will you take next?
